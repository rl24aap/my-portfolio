Gridworld AI Project

I created a Gridworld AI project using Python. The project involved exploring three different environments to understand how artificial intelligence can make decisions and solve problems.

In each environment, an AI agent moves around a grid to reach a specific goal. This helped me understand how artificial intelligence can interact with an environment, make decisions and find a way to reach its target.

Project Objectives

  • Explore three different Gridworld environments
  • Understand how an AI agent interacts with an environment
  • Learn how AI makes decisions
  • Develop Python programming and problem-solving skills

Development and Learning

Through this project, I gained practical experience using Python to explore artificial intelligence. Working with three different Gridworld environments helped me develop my understanding of decision-making, problem-solving and how an AI agent can reach a goal.
